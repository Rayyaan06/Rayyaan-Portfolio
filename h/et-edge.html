About my role

Creative work shaped by the speaker, audience, and business objective.

Since joining ET Edge | India Leadership Council as a Marketing Management Trainee, I have contributed to the planning, branding, and promotion of executive leadership experiences featuring CEOs, founders, policymakers, authors, athletes, and industry leaders.

My role extends beyond graphic design. Every project begins by understanding the speaker, audience, and business objective before translating those insights into a strategic visual narrative.

June 2026

From Behind Bars to Beyond Stigma

Featuring Dr. Kiran Bedi

How do you encourage business leaders to rethink Corporate Social Responsibility beyond philanthropy and view rehabilitation as a long-term societal investment?

The campaign reframed correctional institutions as spaces of transformation rather than confinement. Instead of focusing on incarceration, the visual narrative emphasized rehabilitation, dignity, second chances, skill development, and meaningful reintegration into society.

The objective was to encourage leaders to view social impact beyond donations and recognize the importance of creating employment opportunities and sustainable pathways for people rebuilding their lives.

June 2026

Playing the Long Game

Featuring Rohan Bopanna

How can the mindset of an elite athlete inspire business leaders navigating disruption, innovation, and uncertainty?

Using Rohan Bopanna's professional journey as the central narrative, the campaign explored resilience, continuous improvement, adaptability, and long-term thinking.

The messaging connected elite sports with business leadership, emphasizing that organizations must continually evolve alongside artificial intelligence, emerging technologies, and changing market conditions while maintaining strategic focus.

July 2026

Driving Change: Building Escape Velocity Through Networks

Featuring Santosh Babu

How do you visualize the invisible relationships that determine how organizations truly function?

The campaign explored the informal networks that exist beneath organizational hierarchies. Rather than focusing on reporting structures, the creative highlighted trust, influence, collaboration, and communication as the real drivers of organizational change.

The visual identity reflected interconnected thinking while reinforcing the masterclass theme of enabling sustainable transformation through invisible networks and compelling leadership narratives.
