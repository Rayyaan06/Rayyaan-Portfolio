NIFTY Forecasting

Forecasting and analysis of the NIFTY 50 index over a ten-year period.

PDF Download Back to Projects
Tools usedPython, pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, seaborn, yfinance, ARIMA, Prophet, scikit-learn
MethodologyHistorical data analysis using moving averages, daily returns, volatility checks, visual trend review, Prophet forecasting, and error evaluation.
Overview

This project combines technical indicators, volatility analysis, correlation review, and Prophet-based forecasting to evaluate the NIFTY 50’s behaviour over time.

Business Problem

Market time series are noisy and difficult to forecast without a disciplined analytical workflow. The project seeks to understand trend structure and the limitations of forward prediction.

Objectives
  • Analyse long-run price movement, returns, and volatility.
  • Build a forecasting model using historical data.
  • Evaluate prediction error and residual behaviour.
Results
  • MAE = 1319.75 and RMSE = 1611.29 in the evaluated forecast window.
  • Moving averages and crash analysis clarified the market structure.
  • Residual review highlighted where predictions diverged from actual prices.
Business Impact

The report translates market data into a more disciplined forecasting narrative suitable for investment and analytics discussions.

Key Learnings

Forecasting value comes less from perfect prediction and more from understanding uncertainty, trend, and error.
