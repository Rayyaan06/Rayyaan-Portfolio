Overview

This page brings the full portfolio into one frame and shows how the work spans consumer research, retail strategy, brand management, and financial analytics.

Business Problem

A strong portfolio needs more than isolated artifacts. It needs a clear system that shows range, discipline, and relevance to business roles.

Objectives
  • Show breadth across research, branding, retail, and analytics.
  • Position the portfolio for consulting and strategy hiring contexts.
  • Make individual case studies easy to browse and evaluate.
Results
  • The portfolio covers brand perception, retail execution, forecasting, and dashboarding.
  • Each case study uses a consulting-style structure.
  • The site is built for employers who evaluate clarity and judgment.
Business Impact

The collection creates a unified professional narrative for roles that require analytical thinking and communication.

Key Learnings

Range matters, but narrative coherence matters more. A portfolio should feel like a single strategic voice.
