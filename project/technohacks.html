Overview

This internship report captures the transition from classroom analytics to applied business work, with a strong emphasis on dashboarding, data interpretation, and practical presentation.

Business Problem

Academic projects often isolate the technical mechanics of analytics. This internship required working through incomplete data, ambiguous requirements, and communication to non-technical stakeholders.

Objectives
  • Build practical analytics fluency using real-world datasets.
  • Improve Power BI and dashboard design capabilities.
  • Translate analysis into business-facing outputs.
Results
  • Improved comfort with end-to-end analytics workflows.
  • Strong focus on visual clarity and business alignment.
  • Greater confidence in turning raw data into insight.
Business Impact

The project demonstrates practical readiness for analyst roles where clarity, structure, and stakeholder communication matter.

Key Learnings

A useful dashboard is not the one with the most charts; it is the one that helps a decision-maker act faster.
