The Authenticity Gap

How Gen Z in India’s D2C fashion sector uses authenticity as a decision gate.

ResearchBrand StrategyConsumer Insight
Case summary

Authenticity as a purchase filter

Context

The project examined how younger consumers evaluate trust, originality, and brand credibility before purchasing from D2C fashion labels.

Approach

Survey inputs and qualitative signals were synthesized into a clear insight framework linking authenticity cues to brand preference.

Outcome

The output translates consumer behavior into practical brand and communication implications.

Tools / methods

Survey design, qualitative synthesis, insight mapping, and brand analysis.
