Retail Strategy

Red One Bags

Brand visibility and demand creation strategy for a mid-market handbag retailer on Linking Road, Bandra.

PDF Download Back to Projects
Tools usedObservation, competitor benchmarking, brand mapping, retail analysis, customer behaviour mapping
MethodologyStore observation, competitor benchmarking, customer mapping, and analysis of local retail footfall patterns around Linking Road.
Overview

This live project studies why a high-footfall retail environment does not automatically convert into sales and examines how the brand can sharpen visibility, consistency, and customer engagement.

Business Problem

The business faced low conversion despite location advantages. The project found that internal execution gaps, weak brand architecture, and inconsistent visual cues were more important than macro market pressure alone.

Objectives
  • Audit the brand visibility gap at store level.
  • Map customer behaviour and purchase sequencing around the retail zone.
  • Recommend practical demand creation levers that fit real operating conditions.
Results
  • Weak brand architecture reduced recall and consistency.
  • Nearby apparel and footwear stores created natural demand spillover.
  • Context-driven selling can improve conversion without discounting.
Business Impact

The project reframed low sales as an execution problem, not only a market problem, and proposed a more disciplined branding system.

Key Learnings

Retail conversion improves when the product is positioned as the final styling element in a customer’s already-formed shopping intent.
