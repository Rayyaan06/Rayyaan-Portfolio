Brand Management

Cotton King

A study of brand personality and consumer associations in apparel retail.

Brand ManagementSurveyRetail
Case summary

Brand perception and positioning

Context

The project assessed the way consumers perceive Cotton King across identity, trust, and market fit.

Approach

A structured brand evaluator framework was used to compare expected versus actual consumer associations.

Outcome

The findings support clearer positioning and more disciplined communication decisions.

Tools / methods

Brand asset evaluators, perception analysis, and survey synthesis.
