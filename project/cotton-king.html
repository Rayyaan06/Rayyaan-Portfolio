Brand Management

Cotton King

Brand asset valuator and brand personality study of a cotton apparel brand.

PDF Download Back to Projects
Tools usedBrand asset valuator, survey analysis, descriptive statistics, Excel
MethodologySurvey-based brand assessment with attribute mapping, rating exercises, and comparative perception analysis.
Overview

This live project evaluates how consumers describe Cotton King and which associations shape its brand personality in the market.

Business Problem

The challenge was to understand which qualities were already owned by the brand and where the brand could sharpen its emotional positioning without losing trust.

Objectives
  • Measure brand personality and attribute associations.
  • Identify the words consumers naturally connect with the brand.
  • Use those insights to inform stronger brand communication.
Results
  • Consumers primarily associated the brand with reliable, down-to-earth, and honest attributes.
  • Comfort, value, durability, and fit stood out in attribute mapping.
  • The brand already held a trust-based persona that could be refined, not replaced.
Business Impact

The project shows how a brand can protect its strongest equity signals while improving consistency and modern relevance.

Key Learnings

When a brand already owns trust, the strategic task is disciplined evolution rather than dramatic reinvention.
